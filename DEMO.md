# Demo script

About 8 minutes. Open https://can-i-still-drive.reedahlstrom.workers.dev on a laptop. You get the phone and the specialist's iPad side by side; the one waiting on you is brighter.

Before you start: press the sliders button (bottom right, or the backtick key), choose a result, and press **Start over**. **Clear** is the good-news path. **Time to stop** is the hard path. **Live** measures the audience's own brake tap at the red light.

## At home (phone)

1. **Get started.** The welcome tells Margaret what happens and who sees the result.
2. **Send code.** Her details come from her doctor's invitation. The code fills itself in.
3. **Connect with HealthKey → Continue → Connect 3 sources → Share.** She picks what to share; visit notes and labs start off. Watch the record gather.
4. **Continue**, answer the four questions, **Done.**

## At the hospital

5. Phone: **I'm here**, then tap **Simulator 1**. The iPad lights up.
6. iPad: **Open.** Point out *Needs attention*: the new sleep medicine, the cataract, the close call. On the right, the drives her record points to are already ticked, each with the reason. Untick one or add one to show the specialist is in charge, then **Assign 6 drives**.
7. Phone: *Dana chose 6 drives for you*, each with why in plain words. **Continue → I feel fine → Continue.**
8. iPad: **Start simulator.** Each drive's score lands as it finishes. In Live mode, tap the road or press space when the light turns red to measure the brake reaction. **Stop drive** is there for simulator sickness.
9. Phone: answer the two after-drive questions. iPad: every drive with its score and one-line reason. **Continue to review.**

## The decision

10. iPad: *Needs attention* first, then DriverScore v0 and each drive (tap a drive for what was measured, the rule and the result), then *Why this suggestion*, the logic step by step. Nothing is selected: **the specialist decides; the score only suggests.** Choose the outcome, show *Margaret will see*, tick the in-person box for Pause or Stop, sign with a finger, **Sign and share with Margaret.**
11. Phone: the signed result, with each drive's score in a plain sentence. Clear opens with the dial; Time to stop opens with getting around. Show **Ride with Sarah** (it asks Sarah and waits for her answer), the Health record tab (the result is saved there), and the Family tab (Margaret chooses what Sarah sees).

## Handy

- **Jump to** skips to any step; **2×** speeds up the animations.
- Open **Phone**, **iPad** or **Simulator** in their own windows to put them on separate screens; they stay in sync.
- Everything works offline once loaded.
