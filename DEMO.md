# Demo script

About 8 minutes. Open https://can-i-still-drive.reedahlstrom.workers.dev on a laptop. You get the phone and the specialist's iPad side by side; the one waiting on you is brighter.

Before you start: press the sliders button (bottom right, or the backtick key), choose a result, and press **Start over**. **Clear** is the good-news path. **Time to stop** is the hard path. **Live** scores whatever the audience does on the tablet tests and brake test.

## At home (phone)

1. **Get started.** The welcome tells Margaret what happens and who sees the result.
2. **Send code.** Her details come from her doctor's invitation. The code fills itself in.
3. **Connect with HealthKey → Continue → Connect 3 sources → Share.** She picks what to share; visit notes and labs start off. Watch the record gather.
4. **Continue**, answer the four questions, **Done.**

## At the hospital

5. Phone: **I'm here**, then tap **Simulator 1**. The iPad lights up.
6. iPad: **Open.** Point out *Needs attention*: the new sleep medicine, the cataract, the close call.
7. **Start tablet tests → Start patient mode.** Hand the laptop to someone in the room for one or two tests (connect the circles is the crowd-pleaser), then press and hold *Test x of 5* to end early, or use Jump to.
8. **Hands-on checks:** one tap per item, totals update live. **Continue to simulator.**
9. Phone: **I feel fine → Continue.** iPad: **Start simulator.** In Live mode, tap the road or press space when the light turns red to measure the brake reaction. **Stop drive** is there for simulator sickness.
10. Phone: answer the two after-drive questions. iPad: **Continue to review.**

## The decision

11. iPad: *Needs attention* first, then DriverScore v0 and each domain (tap a row for the items). Nothing is selected: **the specialist decides; the score only suggests.** Choose the outcome, show *Margaret will see*, tick the in-person box for Pause or Stop, sign with a finger, **Sign and share with Margaret.**
12. Phone: the signed result. Clear opens with the dial; Time to stop opens with getting around. Show **Ride with Sarah** (it asks Sarah and waits for her answer), the Health record tab (the result is saved there), and the Family tab (Margaret chooses what Sarah sees).

## Handy

- **Jump to** skips to any step; **2×** speeds up the animations.
- Open **Phone**, **iPad** or **Simulator** in their own windows to put them on separate screens; they stay in sync.
- Everything works offline once loaded.
